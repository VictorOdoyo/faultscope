# Equipment duty comparisons

The comparison uses the largest phase initial RMS current, a three-phase equivalent peak when supported, and a constant-current I²t number. Ratings are supplied independently and compared in kA, kA peak and kA²s. Missing/unsupported comparisons are null, never a fabricated pass. Initial RMS compared with a breaking rating is only a screening proxy; breaker asymmetry, contact-parting time, TRV and complete rating/application rules are outside this tool.
